Frequently Asked Questions

Currently, our system can detect two common potato diseases: Early Blight and Late Blight. It can also identify healthy potato plants. We plan to expand our detection capabilities to include more diseases in future updates.

Our system has been trained on a diverse dataset of potato plant images and achieves an accuracy rate of approximately 90% for the diseases it can detect. The accuracy may vary depending on the quality of the uploaded image and the stage of the disease.

For best results, take clear, well-lit photos of the potato plant leaves. Focus on areas showing symptoms, and try to include multiple affected leaves in the frame. Avoid shadows and make sure the image is not blurry. Photos taken during daylight provide better results than those taken with flash in low light.
