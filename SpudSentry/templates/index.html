Common Potato Diseases

Learn about the most prevalent diseases affecting potato crops

Common

Early Blight

Early blight is caused by the fungus Alternaria solani. It appears as dark brown to black spots with concentric rings, creating a "target-spot" or "bull's-eye" pattern. These spots usually appear on lower, older leaves first.

Key Symptoms:
  • Dark, target-like spots on leaves
  • Yellowing of leaves around lesions
  • Premature defoliation
Severe

Late Blight

Late blight is caused by the water mold Phytophthora infestans. This is the disease that caused the Irish Potato Famine. It appears as pale green, water-soaked lesions that quickly turn brown to black. White fungal growth may appear on leaf undersides in humid conditions.

Key Symptoms:
  • Water-soaked lesions that rapidly enlarge
  • White fuzzy growth on leaf undersides
  • Rapid death of affected tissues

About This Project

PotatoPathFinder is a college project created by Ritika Chaudhary and Veer Vikram Singh as part of their computer science curriculum. This tool combines image processing techniques with machine learning to help farmers and agricultural researchers identify potato diseases quickly and accurately.

The system uses a convolutional neural network trained on thousands of potato plant images to recognize patterns associated with different diseases. The goal is to provide early detection and treatment recommendations to improve crop health and yield.
